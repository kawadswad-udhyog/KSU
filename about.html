Heritage & Passion

Our Story

KAWAD SWAD was founded on a simple yet unyielding passion: to bring the true, uncompromised taste of authentic Jain papad to dining tables across the nation. What began as a dedicated endeavor to preserve traditional culinary heritage has evolved into a trusted symbol of purity and excellence.

Rooted deeply in traditional Jain recipes, our preparation methods adhere to strict dietary principles while honoring time-tested culinary techniques. Every single batch is crafted using carefully selected pulses, pristine spices, and pure ingredients to deliver that classic crunch and rich regional flavor.

By harmonizing time-honored recipes with modern, hygienic manufacturing standards, KAWAD SWAD ensures that every papad reflects our unwavering commitment to quality, authenticity, and absolute trust.

Our Mission

To manufacture and deliver pure, authentic, and premium Jain papads crafted under strict hygiene standards using time-honored recipes—ensuring uncompromised quality and satisfaction in every bite for families everywhere.

Our Vision

To become India’s most respected and widely trusted brand for traditional Jain food products, celebrated for our commitment to purity, culinary heritage, and manufacturing excellence across domestic and international markets.
