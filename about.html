The Purity Standard

Why Commercial Brands Add Rice Flour — And Why We Never Will

Most mass-produced commercial papads cut costs by mixing up to 30–40% cheap rice flour or starch into their dough. While this increases profits, it destroys the authentic snap, leaves a heavy residue, and diminishes digestible protein.

The Kawad Swad Guarantee

We use strictly premium Yellow Moong, Chana, and Urad dal ground fresh in small batches. Combined with authentic Hing (Asafoetida) and coarse black pepper, our papads offer effortless digestion and unmatched aroma.

Craftsmanship

How Our Papads Are Crafted

01

Moath & Dal Selection

Only whole pulses are sourced directly from trusted regional farmers. Pulses are double-cleaned to eliminate grit before slow milling.

02

Traditional Mathai & Hand-Rolling

Local women artisans beat the dough (Mathai) using wooden mallets to achieve maximum elasticity without adding synthetic dough conditioners.

03

Sun-Drying & Moisture Sealing

Papads are dried under clean solar rays in Katargaon and immediately sealed in barrier pouches to lock in natural crispness.
