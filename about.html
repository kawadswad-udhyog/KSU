Our Story & Heritage

Founded on uncompromised quality, hard work, and family values in Katargaon, Nimar.

Katargaon Roots

Built on Daily Consistency & Traditional Gutai

Kawad Swad Udhyog is a dedicated family-run food manufacturing facility based in Katargaon, Nimar (MP). Our story is simple: daily discipline, meticulous raw material selection, and authentic processing methods passed down through generations.

Every papad is a result of thorough Guthai & Mathai. We believe that true regional taste comes from following traditional sun-drying techniques without taking shortcut industrial compromises.

100% Quality Focus Katargaon Facility

Direct Production Control

We maintain 100% in-house supervision over our manufacturing unit. Direct monitoring at every step ensures that each packet delivers the exact same crisp texture and balanced spice profile our customers rely on.

Hygienic Sun-Drying Facility

0% Rice Flour Promise

Authentic Nimari papad should be pure pulse base. We strictly do not use cheap rice flour fillers in our recipes. This commitment ensures superior crunch, high protein quality, and lighter digestion.
