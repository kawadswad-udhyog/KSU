Kitchen Knowledge & Tips

Simple techniques to get the perfect texture and preserve freshness.

Perfect Papad Frying Tips

Oil temperature is everything. Ensure oil is smoking hot before dropping the papad, and press gently with a slotted spoon for 3 seconds for uniform expansion without curling.

Master Class

Roasting vs Frying

Roasting over an open flame enhances pepper notes and offers a lower-calorie crunch. Deep frying brings out the rich pulse taste and creates maximum crispness for gravy pairings.

Comparison Guide

Papad Storage Guide

Keep raw papads in an airtight container away from moisture. Adding a few cloves or dry bay leaves inside the storage jar keeps them fresh and prevents dampness during monsoons.
