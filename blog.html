Mastering the Art of Jain Papad Preparation

Discover time-tested regional techniques for roasting, frying, and seasoning authentic Jain papads while preserving their crisp texture and pure flavor profile.

The Secret to the Perfect Crisp

Preparing traditional papad requires careful attention to heat and timing. Whether open-flame roasting or light pan frying, keeping the heat uniform prevents scorching while unlocking the aromatic spices blended inside moong, urad, or chana dal variants.

Serving & Pairings

Enhance everyday meals by pairing crispy papads with classic Indian main courses or topping them with fresh coriander and authentic Jain seasonings for an appetizing starter.
