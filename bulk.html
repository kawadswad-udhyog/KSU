Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing Kawad Swad products in bulk.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)? +

Our wholesale minimum order quantities are optimized for commercial partners. Please contact us via the enquiry form to get specific MOQs tailored to your region and product mix.

What is the typical delivery timeframe? +

Standard dispatch occurs within 2-4 business days post order confirmation. Delivery times vary between 3 to 7 business days depending on destination logistics.

What are the available payment terms? +

Initial orders are processed on standard advance or bank transfer terms. Credit arrangements can be discussed for long-term commercial partners based on order history.

Which product variants are available for bulk purchase? +

Our complete Jain papad product line including Moong, Chana, Urad, Specialty Masala variants, and Combo Packs are available for wholesale supply.

What packaging options are available? +

We provide standard commercial bulk cartons engineered to prevent transit damage, preserving product freshness and structural integrity.

How is business support managed post-order? +

Every wholesale client is assigned a dedicated business manager for order tracking, re-ordering, invoice requests, and logistical queries.
