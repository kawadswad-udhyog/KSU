What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Standard dispatch occurs within 2-4 business days post order confirmation. Delivery times vary between 3 to 7 business days depending on destination logistics.

Initial orders are processed on standard advance or bank transfer terms. Credit arrangements can be discussed for long-term commercial partners based on order history.
