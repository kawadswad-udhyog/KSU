FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers regarding distributor, wholesale, and private label operations

How do I become an authorized KAWAD SWAD distributor?

You can apply by filling out the business enquiry form on this page selecting 'Distributor' as your business type. Our commercial sales team will review your market reach and get in touch within 24–48 hours.

What are the requirements for wholesale purchasing?

Wholesale buyers require a valid GST/Business Registration. We offer competitive tiered pricing depending on the volume ordered for standardized product lines.

Do you provide complete contract manufacturing for Private Labeling?

Yes, we provide end-to-end OEM and private label manufacturing services including customized recipe formulations, sizing options, and complete packaging execution under strict NDA protocols.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs vary based on the model: Wholesale standard orders start at modest carton thresholds, while Private Label and custom formulations require higher production run minimums.

What are the typical production and dispatch lead times?

Standard wholesale orders are dispatched within 3 to 7 business days. Initial private label runs typically take 2 to 3 weeks including packaging approvals and printing setup.

What packaging options are available?

We offer standard pouches, bulk master cartons, and custom laminated structures engineered for enhanced shelf life and export standards.

How is freight and shipping handled?

We partner with national logistics networks to offer reliable door-to-door or ex-factory transport solutions tailored to your operational requirements.

What marketing and operational support is provided?

Authorized distributors receive promotional assets, product collateral, sample units, and dedicated account support for ongoing orders and supply chain management.
