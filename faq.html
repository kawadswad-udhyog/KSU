Products & Dietary Standards

What product variants does Kawad Swad manufacture?

We manufacture 100% vegetarian Jain papads in Moong, Chana, Urad, and Specialty Masala variants available in 200g, 235g, 500g, and 1000g retail and bulk pack sizes.

Are all products strictly Jain vegetarian?

Yes. Every recipe adheres strictly to traditional Jain dietary guidelines, using pure pulses and spices without any prohibited ingredients or artificial flavor enhancers.
