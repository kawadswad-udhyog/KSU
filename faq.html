Products FAQ

We manufacture authentic Indian FMCG food products, specializing in premium handmade papads, traditional pickles, namkeen, and authentic spices crafted using traditional family recipes.
Our papad range includes classic Moong Dal Papad, Udad Dal Special, Chana Dal Papad, Kali Mirch (Black Pepper) Special, Garlic Papad, and Punjabi Masala Papad in various sizes.
Yes, 100% of our products are certified pure vegetarian. They are manufactured in a strict vegetarian facility compliant with high food safety standards.
We prioritize natural preservation techniques like sun-drying and traditional oil immersion. Our products contain zero artificial preservatives, chemical colors, or synthetic flavors.
Store papad in a cool, dry place inside an airtight container. Keep away from direct sunlight, moisture, and humidity to preserve freshness and crispness.
Most of our papad and snack items have a shelf life of 6 to 12 months from the date of manufacturing when stored according to recommendations.

Shipping
