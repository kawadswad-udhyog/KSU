Our Core Philosophy

Crafted with Care, Guided by Quality

At Kawad Swad, our manufacturing philosophy brings together the richness of traditional Jain recipes with the precision of modern production techniques. We believe that exceptional taste begins long before the rolling process—it starts with the uncompromising integrity of our sourcing and environment.

Operating within a pristine, climate-controlled setup, we maintain absolute hygiene at every single touchpoint. From carefully selecting premium quality pulses and spices to ensuring consistent thickness and automated, moisture-controlled drying, every step is rigorously monitored.

This seamless harmony between authentic heritage methods and disciplined modern quality control ensures that every batch delivers the exact crunch, purity, and flavor our customers and business partners rely upon.

Step-By-Step Precision

Manufacturing Process

A meticulous 7-step journey ensuring unmatched freshness, authentic texture, and premium quality in every single batch.

Step 01

Ingredient Selection

Hand-picked premium lentils and pure spices thoroughly tested for quality.

Step 02

Mixing

Proportional dry blending of pulse flours and traditional Jain seasoning.

Step 03

Dough Preparation

Kneaded to exact elasticity using pure water under strict hygienic conditions.

Step 04

Papad Rolling

Precision rolling to maintain uniform thickness and perfect round shape.

Step 05

Drying

Controlled moisture removal ensuring optimal crispness without degradation.

Step 06

Quality Inspection

Multi-point visual and taste checks to reject any defective batch.

Step 07

Packaging

Sealed in moisture-barrier silver pouches for long-lasting freshness.
