Brand Overview

Company Introduction

Authentic Taste, Uncompromising Quality

Who We Are

KAWAD SWAD is a premier food and spices manufacturing brand committed to delivering authentic traditional flavors through modern, hygienic manufacturing standards.

What We Manufacture

We specialize in a comprehensive range of premium spices, authentic blended masalas, traditional seasonings, and everyday culinary essentials crafted for households and commercial kitchens alike.

What Makes Us Different

Our rigorous multi-stage quality checks, state-of-the-art processing facility, direct sourcing from trusted agrarian partners, and zero-compromise purity ethos set us apart in the fast-growing FMCG sector.

Brand Vision

To become a globally recognized benchmark for culinary purity and authentic taste, bringing traditional flavor perfection into every home across the nation and beyond.

Brand Mission

To consistently innovate and manufacture high-quality, pure, and safe food products using sustainable sourcing, advanced technologies, and customer-first values.

Brand Assets Usage

Maintain clear margin space. Do not alter colors, stretch, rotate, or add unauthorized shadows/effects.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for media representatives and press members.

How do I submit media and press questions?

You can send all press inquiries, commentary requests, and publication questions directly to our media desk at [media@kawadswad.com]. Our team responds within 24 business hours.

How do I request photography or video permissions?

High-res imagery inside our Download Center is pre-approved for editorial print and digital reporting. For custom video shoots or high-resolution factory access, please submit a request to our media desk.

How do I schedule an executive interview?

Interview requests for executive leadership should include the publication name, interview topic, proposed deadline, and format (email, phone, or in-person). Contact [media@kawadswad.com].

Where can I download the official Press Kit?

Our complete Press Kit is available in the Download Center section above. It includes corporate history, founder biographies, and key factsheets.

Are there guidelines for using Brand Assets?

Yes, all asset usage must comply with our Brand Guidelines. Vector logos must not be stretched or recolored. Please refer to our Brand Assets Usage section on this page.

Need Official Information?

Our media team will be happy to assist you with interviews, press enquiries, product photography and official brand resources.
