Brand Overview

Company Introduction

Authentic Taste, Uncompromising Quality

Who We Are

KAWAD SWAD Udhyog is an established Jain papad manufacturer in the Nimar region of Madhya Pradesh, committed to delivering traditional regional flavor through hygienic manufacturing.

What We Manufacture

We specialize in 100% vegetarian Jain papads including Moong, Chana, Urad, and Specialty Masala variants crafted for retail households and wholesale buyers.

What Makes Us Different

Touchless automated processing, strict Jain dietary compliance, FSSAI quality verification (Lic No. 21425890001224), and protective barrier packaging set our products apart.

Brand Vision

To become a nationally recognized leader in traditional Jain food products, bringing clean, authentic flavor into every Indian home with trust and transparency.

Brand Mission

To manufacture pure, high-quality, and 100% vegetarian food items through sustainable sourcing, hygienic automation, and customer-first service.
