Shipping Policy

Order Processing

All orders are freshly prepared and dispatched within 24–48 hours after confirmation.

Delivery Locations

We deliver pan-India to over 19,000+ pin codes through reliable express couriers.

Estimated Time

Metro Cities: 2–4 business days.
Rest of India: 4–7 business days.

Shipping Partners

We partner with premier logistics providers including BlueDart, Delhivery, and India Post.

Shipping Charges

Free shipping on orders above ₹499. Nominal flat fee of ₹50 applies to orders below ₹499.

Secure Packaging

Sealed in vacuum-grade food safe packs and sturdy outer boxes to preserve peak freshness.

Return & Refund Policy

As KAWAD SWAD offers perishable food items, returns are accepted exclusively if you receive a damaged, tampered, or incorrect item. Please report the issue with unboxing photos/videos within 24 hours of delivery.

Once verified and approved, refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method or issued as store credit instantly.

If eligible, a fresh replacement dispatch is initiated within 24 hours at no extra cost to you.

Returns/refunds are not applicable for personal taste preferences, incorrect delivery addresses provided by the customer, or issues reported after 24 hours of delivery.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation Before Dispatch

Orders can be cancelled free of charge if requested within 2 hours of placing the order or before dispatch confirmation.

Cancellation After Dispatch

Once dispatched, orders cannot be cancelled as perishable food items are packed and handed over to transit partners.

How to Request Cancellation

Contact us immediately via WhatsApp or call us with your Order ID to request a cancellation.

Privacy Policy

We collect essential details like your name, phone number, shipping address, and email strictly to process and deliver your orders accurately. We never sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your data is protected using SSL encryption standard protocols. Payment processing is handled securely via RBI-compliant payment gateways; KAWAD SWAD never stores your payment credentials.

We use essential cookies to maintain your shopping cart sessions and improve website navigation performance.
