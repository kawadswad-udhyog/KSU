Frequently Asked Questions

Are all Kawad Swad products 100% Jain compliant?

Yes, all our products are strictly manufactured following Jain dietary guidelines without any root vegetables or non-compliant additives.

What packaging sizes are available for commercial supply?

We offer standard retail packs of 200g, 500g, and 1000g, along with customized bulk packaging options for commercial partners.

What is the shelf life of Kawad Swad papads?

Our papads have a shelf life of 6 to 9 months when stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

How can I partner with Kawad Swad for distribution?

You can reach out through our Business page enquiry forms, and our sales team will connect with you within 24–48 hours.
