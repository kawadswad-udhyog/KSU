Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our review process and authentic quality.

How are customer reviews verified on Kawad Swad?

Reviews with the "Verified Purchase" badge are collected automatically from customers who completed an order directly through our official channels.

Can I upload photos and videos with my review?

Yes! We encourage customers to upload photos and short unboxing or preparation videos using our "Write a Review" form.

How long does it take for a submitted review to appear?

Submitted reviews are moderated within 24-48 hours to ensure content guidelines and media uploads adhere to safety and authenticity standards.

What if I received a damaged or incorrect package?

If you experience any fulfillment issues, please contact our dedicated support team immediately through the contact page for quick resolution before leaving product feedback.

Are negative reviews published?

We value complete transparency. All genuine reviews from verified buyers are published provided they conform to our community guidelines.
