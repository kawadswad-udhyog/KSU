What happens after I submit my application?
Our talent acquisition team reviews every submission within 3 to 5 business days. If your qualification matches our requirements, a recruiter will reach out for an initial screening call.

Yes, freshers are welcome to apply for entry-level roles across production, sales, content, and warehouse operations. We value dedication, adaptability, and enthusiasm.
