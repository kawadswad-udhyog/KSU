Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting your career with KAWAD SWAD.

What happens after I submit my application?
Our talent acquisition team reviews every submission within 3 to 5 business days. If your qualification matches our requirements, a recruiter will reach out for an initial screening call.
Are freshers eligible to apply for open positions?
Yes, freshers are welcome to apply for entry-level roles across production, sales, content, and warehouse operations. We value dedication, adaptability, and enthusiasm.
Where are KAWAD SWAD work locations situated?
Our central headquarters and primary production manufacturing units are located in state-of-the-art industrial facilities, while our sales team operates across multiple regional territories.
What is the typical timeline for the complete interview process?
The entire selection process generally takes between 1 to 2 weeks from initial screening to final offer generation, depending on the position level.
Can I apply for multiple roles simultaneously?
We recommend applying for the single position that best matches your primary skill set and career aspirations to ensure efficient review.
How can I submit my profile if there are no matching openings currently?
You can select "Other / General Application" in the Application Form and upload your details. Our HR team maintains active candidate pools for upcoming expansions.
